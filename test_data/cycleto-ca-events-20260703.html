FIFA Bike Valet


Did you know that football fans can take advantage of FREE bike parking with Cycle Toronto Bike Valet? Skip the traffic and bike with peace of mind!

Bike Valet opens one hour before and closes one hour after gates (subject to change).

All Fan Festival days (June 10 until July 19):

  • Inukshuk (789 Lake Shore Blvd W, Toronto)

Match days (June 12, June 17, June 20, June 23, June 26, July 2):

  • Inukshuk (789 Lake Shore Blvd W, Toronto)
  • Hanna (15 Hanna Ave, Toronto)
  • Ordnance (801 Wellington St W, Toronto)

Find out more: torontofwc26.ca/getting-around

FAQ

What does it cost?

Cycle Toronto Bike Valet is free! But if you appreciate the service, consider becoming a member to support our work.

Can I leave my helmet and bag?

Yes, we can keep it all with your bike.

Do I need a lock?

No, your bike is kept under the watchful eyes of our attendants in our secure perimeter.

How can I get Bike Valet for my event?

Contact us: cycleto.ca/bikevalet

Rec Hub DIY

Rec Hub at Evergreen Brick Works, 550 Bayview Avenue

The Cycle Toronto Rec Hub at Evergreen Brick Works is a welcoming community space that takes the intimidation out of bike maintenance and repair.

IF YOU'RE ALREADY A TRAINED CYCLE TORONTO REC HUB VOLUNTEER, SIGN UP FOR SUPPORT SHIFTS.

Our friendly staff and volunteers will help guide you through the basics to keep you rolling. Even expert home mechanics are also welcome to use our facilities.

DIY service is first come, first serve.

Find out more: cycleto.ca/rec_hub

Engagement Station - Willowdale Summer Environment Day 2026

Centrepoint Mall North Parking Lot, 6464 Yonge St

We are hosting a Bike Engagement Station at Willowdale Summer Environment Day on Saturday, July 11. We'll be there with some educational materials and will demonstrate how to perform safety checks for those who may not have access to a bike shop but want to get riding. We'll be able to help with things like inflating your tires, lubricating your chain, demonstrating how to do an ABC safety check, and anything else our teams can safely accomplish in a few minutes.

See the details below:

  • Date: Saturday, July 11, 2026
  • Time: 10:00 am - 2:00 pm
  • Location: 6464 Yonge St, North York M2M 3X4 (Center Point Mall Parking Lot)

Bike Month is a month long celebration of cycling across Toronto. Join us on community rides, learn more about the joys of cycling or visit one of our bike engagement stations. Visit toronto.ca/bikemonth.

See you there!
