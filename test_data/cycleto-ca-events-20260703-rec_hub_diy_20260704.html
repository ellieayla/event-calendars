The Cycle Toronto Rec Hub at Evergreen Brick Works is a welcoming community space that takes the intimidation out of bike maintenance and repair.

IF YOU'RE ALREADY A TRAINED CYCLE TORONTO REC HUB VOLUNTEER, SIGN UP FOR SUPPORT SHIFTS.

Our friendly staff and volunteers will help guide you through the basics to keep you rolling. Even expert home mechanics are also welcome to use our facilities.

DIY service is first come, first serve.
