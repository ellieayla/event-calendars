Eco-Ride Series with Environment Hamilton

When you ride a bike, do you notice your environment more? Are you curious about the ecological story of Hamilton?  We're launching a special Group Ride series in partnership with Environment Hamilton this summer. Each ride will focus on a particular layer of environmentalism in the context of our city, with routes and teaching oriented toward a specific theme. It's a great way to learn more about Hamilton's unique ecological history and challenges, all from the seat of a bicycle.
Join us for the following two rides:
🌳🏙️
Park to Park
On Tuesday July 28 Environment Hamilton and Hamilton Bike Share are hosting a Park to Park Bike Tour leaving from the Beasley Park Bike Share Hub. The ride will be just over 10 km with stops at four different parks and will end by 8pm. In addition to Beasley Park, riders will stop at Dundurn Castle, Churchill Park, Victoria Park, and will discuss green space access as it relates to demographics in different areas of the city.
📆Tuesday, July 28

⏰6:30PM-8:00PM

📍Beasley Park Bike Share Hub (Elgin St at Cannon St E)

Industrial Impacts, Past & Present
On Thursday August 20, Environment Hamilton and Hamilton Bike Share are hosting an Industrial Impacts Past & Present Cycling Tour leaving from the Bayfront Park Bike Share Hub. The ride will be just over 10 km with stops and will end by 8pm. Cycling tour leads will discuss the impacts of industry, legacy pollution, and waste dumping.
📆Thursday, August 20

⏰6:30pm - 8:00pm

📍Bayfront Park bike share hub (Bay St N at Strachan St W under the Waterfront Trail sail post)
